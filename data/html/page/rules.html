Rules

Before you start solving, please take a moment to read the rules.

The basics

  • Puzzles unlock at scheduled times.
  • Solve puzzles in any order.
  • Each puzzle has two parts. Part 1 must be solved before part 2 is available.
  • Locked puzzles will appear greyed out until they're available.

Performance and precision

  • Every puzzle is solvable using 64-bit integers - no intermediate or final value will overflow.
  • An efficient solution to all puzzles runs in under a few seconds on modest hardware. If your program takes much longer, your approach is probably not the intended one.

Submissions

  • Each puzzle will tell you what kind of answer to submit (a number, a word, etc.).
  • Enter your answer on the puzzle's page.
  • You'll immediately see whether it's correct - no partial credit or hints are given.

Leaderboard

  • See the leaderboard page for a detailed breakdown of how positions are calculated.

Fair play

  • Work on your own solutions - collaboration or code sharing is not allowed.
  • Don't brute-force or spam the answer form.
  • Do not use large language models (LLMs) or automated solvers to generate or verify answers.
  • Be kind and respectful in any community discussions.

Questions?

If something seems unclear, reach out to us.
